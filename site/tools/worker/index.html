Why a Worker at all?
  • The reader takes at most 128 KB per download and has little memory. Feeds and web pages come as full HTML; the Worker turns them into short plain text and pages the lists.
  • Your keys stay off the reader: the CommaFeed API key and the full-text services' tokens are Cloudflare secrets. The reader only knows the Worker's address and a token or password of its own.
  • Full text: feeds often carry only a summary. The Worker asks a full-text service, falls back to the feed, and keeps the result for a day.
  • It also serves an OPDS catalog of your feeds to the reader's own library, KOReader and others. It costs nothing and only you use it.
