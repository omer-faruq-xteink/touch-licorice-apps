Anki decks

Turn an Anki deck or a simple word list into the card files the Anki app on the reader studies. The same conversion as apkg2cards.py, without installing anything.

  1. Add a deck: an .apkg exported from Anki (desktop or mobile) or downloaded from AnkiWeb's shared decks; or a list with one card per line, front and back separated by a tab (.tsv, .txt) or a comma (.csv).

    Drop a deck here, or click to choose one .apkg, .tsv, .csv, .txt

  2. Choose what goes on each side. The defaults are the deck's first field with text on the front and the others on the back.

    Updating a deck you already study?

    Add the files of the earlier conversion (the zip from this page, or the prefix_info.txt and prefix_c000.txt … files from the reader). Cards keep their places, so your progress is kept; removed cards are blanked and new ones go at the end.

    Drop the earlier files or their zip none added

  3. Unzip the download and copy the files into AppFiles/anki/ on the reader's SD card (USB mode). The deck shows up in Anki's list. A copy of Anki installed by hand from anki.xtapp reads XTApps/anki/files/; Anki's Menu → Help shows the folder.

Only text is kept: pictures and sounds are left out (the reader has no sound), which is why a 200 MB deck usually becomes a few hundred kilobytes. Cloze cards show [...] on the front and the full text on the back. Reading .apkg files loads sql.js (and, for newer exports, fzstd) from a public CDN; your deck stays in this browser.
