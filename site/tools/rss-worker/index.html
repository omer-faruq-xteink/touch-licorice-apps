RSS Worker setup

The RSS app reads your CommaFeed articles through a small server of your own, the rss-opds Worker, on Cloudflare's free plan. This page walks through it and works out what to type on the reader.

Why a Worker at all?
  • The reader takes at most 128 KB per download and has little memory. CommaFeed sends articles as full HTML; the Worker turns them into short plain text and pages the lists.
  • Your keys stay off the reader: the CommaFeed API key and the full-text services' tokens are Cloudflare secrets. The reader only knows the Worker's address and a password or token of its own.
  • Full text: feeds often carry only a summary. The Worker asks a full-text service, falls back to the feed, and keeps the result for a day.
  • One Worker serves the RSS, Hacker News and Readwise apps, and an OPDS catalog for the reader's own library, KOReader and others. It costs nothing and only you use it.

  1. A CommaFeed API key. In CommaFeed open Settings → Profile, generate an API key and keep it at hand. (CommaFeed makes a new one when you change your password.)

  2. A Cloudflare account, free, no card: dash.cloudflare.com. And Node.js 18 or later, and git, on your computer.

  3. Your choices. The reader signs in to the Worker with a path token (typed once in the app) or with a user name and password.

    You choose the password when the command below asks for it; it is not entered on this page.
