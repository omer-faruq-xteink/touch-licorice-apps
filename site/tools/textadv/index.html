Text adventures

The Text adventures app plays Z-machine story files: the Infocom classics and most games written in Inform. Type little: directions and common commands are buttons, the game's words from its last reply are chips to tap, and the keyboard is there for the rest.

Zork I, II and III

Microsoft released the three Zork games under the MIT licence in 2025. Zork I is built into the app; the pack has all three, for Zork II and III.

Download the Zork pack (three .z3 files, about 270 KB)

  1. Unzip it and copy the .z3 files into AppFiles/textadv/ on the reader's SD card (USB mode).

  2. Open Text adventures and tap a game. Start with Zork I: "open mailbox".

More games

  • Any .z3, .z5 or .z8 story file works (not .z6, which needs pictures, and not Glulx .ulx / .gblorb).
  • Thousands of games are on the IF Archive, described and rated on IFDB (search for "Z-code" games). Each game has its own terms; most are free to download and play.
  • A game in a .zblorb file: many sites also offer the plain .z5 / .z8 file; take that one.

Playing

  • Your place is saved after every turn: leave the app and it continues there. The game's own SAVE and RESTORE keep one more save per game.
  • Tap the left third of the text to page back, the rest to page on; the side buttons do the same.
  • Menu: text size, restart, back to the list of games.
