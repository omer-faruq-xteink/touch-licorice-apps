StarDict dictionaries

The Dictionary app reads small sorted files, one per lookup, so a StarDict dictionary is cut into pieces once, here. The same conversion as stardict2shards.py, without installing anything.

  1. Add the dictionary: its .ifo, .idx and .dict or .dict.dz files together, or the archive they came in (.zip, .tar.gz, .tgz, .tar.zst, .tar).

    Drop the files or the archive here, or click to choose .ifo + .idx + .dict(.dz), or an archive

  2. Give each dictionary its own prefix (letters and digits, such as entr) so several can share the app's folder. The folder holds 512 files: keep the total under about 500 (a larger piece size makes fewer files).

  3. Unzip the download and copy the files into AppFiles/dict/ on the reader's SD card (USB mode). A copy of Dictionary installed by hand from dict.xtapp reads XTApps/dict/files/; with no dictionary yet, the app shows its folder.

HTML dictionaries are reduced to text; dictd-style plain text is unwrapped with one sense per line; pictures and sounds inside entries are left out. .tar.zst archives load fzstd from a public CDN; your files stay in this browser.
