Piano scores

Piano Follow shows sheet music on the reader and follows your playing. Pieces from its catalog download on the reader; here you can add your own: the music is engraved for the reader's screen and written to its card. Nothing is uploaded.

  1. Add a score as MusicXML (.musicxml, .xml, .mxl), which MuseScore, Sibelius, Finale and Dorico can export, or ABC (.abc).

    Drop the file here, or click to choose .musicxml, .xml, .mxl or .abc

  3. With the reader in USB mode, Write to the reader asks once for the card (choose the card itself) and puts the file in AppFiles/pianofollow/ (Chrome or Edge on a computer). Or download it and copy it there yourself.

MIDI files, PDFs and photos of sheet music cannot be converted: notation guessed from MIDI reads badly, and reading notes from images is a different problem. Use files you have the right to use; they stay on your computer and your reader. The engraver is Verovio (LGPL), loaded from a public CDN.
