Offline maps

Offline Map shows a map on the reader without a connection, like a folding paper map. Make a map pack of an area here: streets and paths with their names, water, parks and forests, drawn in black and white for e-ink. Or make a route to follow on it. Everything is made in this browser, and no account is needed.

    1. Choose the area. Press Draw area and drag a rectangle on the map, or use what the map shows.

    2. Choose the detail.

    3. Make the pack.

    4. Put it on the reader. With the reader in USB mode, Copy to reader asks once for the card (choose the card itself) and puts the pack in AppFiles/offlinemap/ (Chrome or Edge on a computer). Or download it and copy it there yourself. Then open Offline Map.

      The buttons appear here when the pack is made.

    1. Draw the route. Press Draw route and click on the map at each turn, or load a GPX from another app. The points are joined by straight lines: click at every bend of the road.

    2. Make the route. The names of the streets it follows are looked up in the map data, so the reader can write them beside the route.

    3. Put it on the reader. Copy to reader puts the route (.gpx) in AppFiles/offlinemap/; in Offline Map it is under Menu, Routes. The reader needs a map pack of the area too: switch to A map pack and press Use route area.

      The buttons appear here when the route is made.
