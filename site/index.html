    How to install

    1. Copy it to the reader

      Connect the reader in USB mode and press Copy to reader (Chrome or Edge on a computer), or download the .xtapp and copy it to the SD card yourself.

    2. Install it on the reader

      Open All Files, tap the package, then Install. An update installs over the app and keeps its settings: do not uninstall first.

    3. Open it

      The apps are in the Ink App row of the drawer. Each one has a help screen.

    The packages are those of the latest release; What’s new lists what changed in each release. Many apps are also in Xteink's app store, where updates arrive by themselves. Tools convert decks, dictionaries and stories for the apps.
