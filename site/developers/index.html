Add your app to the list

Made an app for the Xteink X4 Pro or X4 Classic? Put it on GitHub with a topic and one small file, and it shows up under Community apps by the next day, with your name and a link to your repo.

Three steps

  1. Publish it on GitHub

    A public repo with your app's .xtapp: in a release, or committed in the repo.

  2. Add the topic

    On the repo's page: the gear next to About, then add the topic licorice-ink-app.

  3. Add inkapp.json

    At the root of the repo's default branch, in the format below. The list is rebuilt once a day.

inkapp.json

{
  "format": 1,
  "apps": [
    {
      "app_id": "reversi",
      "name": "Reversi",
      "version": "1.2.0",
      "description": "The classic disc-flipping game against the reader, three levels.",
      "devices": ["X4 Pro", "X4 Classic"],
      "category": "games",
      "package": "https://github.com/you/ink-games/releases/latest/download/reversi.xtapp",
      "icon": "icons/reversi.png",
      "screenshots": ["shots/reversi-1.png"],
      "homepage": "https://github.com/you/ink-games",
      "license": "MIT"
    }
  ]
}
field
formatAlways 1.
appsOne entry per app; a repo can list up to 20.
app_idThe app_id of your manifest: lower-case letters only.
name, versionUp to 40 characters; a dotted version such as 1.2.0. Keep it the same as in your package.
descriptionOne or two sentences, up to 200 characters.
devices"X4 Pro" (touch), "X4 Classic" (keys), or both.
categoryOptional: read, tools, learn, stories, daily, puzzles or games (default tools).
packageWhere the .xtapp is: an https:// address (a release download works well) or a path in the repo.
icon, screenshotsOptional pictures, as paths in the repo or https:// addresses: a square icon, and up to 6 screenshots.
firmwareOptional: the oldest firmware build it needs, such as "26110201".
homepage, licenseOptional: a page for the app (default: the repo), and its license.

Paths are read from the repo's default branch. An entry with a missing or wrong field is left out; the others in the file still count.

Good to know

  • The site does not copy, host or check your package: visitors download it from your repo, and the list says so.
  • Repos are listed by stars, then by name. Your GitHub name and repo are shown with each app.
  • To leave the list, remove the topic. Repos that cause trouble can be left out by the site's maintainer.
  • Xteink also has its own app store, with review: xtapp-ai.xteink.cc.

Questions or a problem with your listing: open an issue.
