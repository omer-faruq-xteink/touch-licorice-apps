== jetty
The boat that brought you turns back into the rain before you have both feet on the jetty. You are the new relief keeper of Gull Rock, and you are a day early.
The lighthouse above you should be turning its beam over the sea. It is dark.
* [once] Look out to sea ~ moves += 1 -> sea
* Walk up to the keeper's cottage ~ moves += 1 -> cottage
* Go straight to the tower ~ moves += 1 -> tower_door

== sea
Far to the south, two small lights rise and fall: a ship, and it is coming this way. Between it and the rock lies the reef the lighthouse is there to warn about.
Someone has to light the lamp, and soon.
-> jetty

== cottage
{cottage == 1: The cottage door is open and bangs in the wind. Inside, a fire has burned down to grey ash. Nobody answers when you call.|The cottage is as cold and empty as before.}
{lantern: Your lantern lights the room.|A storm lantern hangs by the door, still warm.}
* {not lantern} Take the lantern ~ lantern = true -> cottage
* [once] Read the note on the table -> note
* [once] Search the dresser drawers ~ moves += 1 -> drawers
* [once] Look on the mantelpiece -> mantel
* Go to the tower ~ moves += 1 -> tower_door

== note
The note is in a large, careful hand:
"Gone down to see to the fog bell. Back by six. Tom."
The clock on the wall says ten past ten.
-> cottage

== drawers
String, candle ends, a tin of buttons. In the bottom drawer, under a folded flag, a heavy iron key.
~ key = true
-> cottage

== mantel
Behind a photograph of a man with a white beard and a dog, you find a box of matches. It rattles: plenty left.
~ matches = true
-> cottage

== tower_door
The tower door is iron, painted white, and locked.
{key: The key from the dresser is heavy in your pocket.}
* {key} Unlock the door ~ moves += 1 -> stairs
* {not key} Break the little window beside it ~ moves += 2; bruises += 1 -> window
* Go back to the cottage ~ moves += 1 -> cottage

== window
The glass gives way at the third blow, and so does the skin on your knuckles. You climb in, slowly and carefully.
-> stairs

== stairs
{lantern: The lantern throws your shadow huge up the walls.|It is pitch dark. You feel for each step with your foot.}
{not lantern: {~You bang your shin on the rail.|You miss a step and land hard on your hands.}}
~ {not lantern} bruises += 1
~ moves += 1
* Climb on to the lamp room -> lamp_check
* [once] Try the door to the store room -> store
* {bruises >= 3} Sit down for a moment -> rest

== rest
You sit on the cold step and count your bruises: {bruises} of them. Your hands are shaking. The wind howls round the tower.
~ moves += 2
-> stairs

== store
Drums of paraffin stand along the wall. You fill the small can by the door.
~ oil = 3
~ moves += 1
-> stairs

== lamp_check
-> {moves > 12} too_late
-> lamp_room

== lamp_room
The lamp room is all glass and brass. In the middle sits the great lens, and inside it the burner, cold.
{oil > 0: The can of paraffin sloshes at your side.|You tap the burner's tank. It rings hollow: empty.}
* {oil > 0 and matches} Fill the tank and light the burner -> lit
* {oil > 0 and not matches} Look for something to light it with -> no_flame
* {oil == 0} Go down for paraffin ~ moves += 1 -> stairs

== no_flame
There is nothing here to strike a flame with. You think of the box of matches in the cottage, a long way down and across the yard.
* Run for the matches ~ moves += 3; matches = true -> lamp_check

== lit
The wick catches. The lens gathers the small flame into a beam as thick as a tree, and the machinery starts its slow turn.
Out at sea, the two lights of the ship stop. Then, slowly, they swing away from the reef.
* Look for Tom at the fog bell -> bell
* Stay with the lamp until morning -> morning

== bell
Down at the fog bell, at the bottom of the rocks, a lantern lies on its side. Beside it, Tom is sitting against the wall with his ankle twisted under him, soaked and furious.
"Two hours I've been shouting," he says. "Who in the world are you?"
You help him up the path, one step at a time, while the beam goes round above you.
-> best_end

== best_end
By morning the storm has blown out. Tom drinks his tea with his foot up on a chair and tells the coastguard on the radio that the new keeper will do.
-> END

== morning
You keep the lamp burning until the sky goes grey. When the morning boat comes, they find Tom down by the fog bell with a twisted ankle, cold but alive.
The ship got home. You never stopped watching the lamp.
-> END

== too_late
You reach the lamp room in time to see it happen: the ship's lights stop, tilt, and go out on the reef.
The lifeboat finds the crew in the morning, clinging to the rocks. Nobody died. But nobody on Gull Rock will forget the night the light was dark.
