# Offline Map

A map of any area of the world on the reader, without a connection: like a folding paper map in your
pocket. Make a map pack on the site's Tools page, copy it to the card, and look at it for days, in
sunlight, on almost no battery. For city walks and hikes alike.

## Making a map pack

On the [Offline maps](https://omer-faruq-xteink.github.io/touch-licorice-apps/tools/offlinemap/) tool
page (Chrome or Edge on a computer):

1. Find a place and drag a rectangle on the map (**Draw area**), or use what the map shows.
2. Choose the detail:
   - **Overview** (zoom 8–13): a region or a small country.
   - **Streets** (zoom 10–17): a city or a hiking area; every street and path, with names.
   - **Close-up** (zoom 10–19): a city centre or a village; buildings, house numbers, every footpath.
3. **Make**. A bar shows the tiles done and the time left; **Cancel** stops, and Make again continues
   where it stopped.
4. With the reader in USB mode, **Copy to reader** puts the pack in `AppFiles/offlinemap/`. Or download
   it and copy it there yourself.

A pack holds at most 50,000 tiles; a bigger area can be several packs. Several packs work side by side:
an overview of a country and detailed packs of two cities, say. On each part of the screen the app
uses the most detailed pack that has it.

The map data is OpenStreetMap, as vector tiles from [VersaTiles](https://versatiles.org/) (no account
or key needed), drawn in black and white for e-ink in your browser.

## On the reader

- **Drag** to move the map; **double tap** to zoom in there; **−** and **+** zoom on the middle; the
  arrow keys move half a screen.
- The header shows the pack, the zoom level and a scale bar. Zoom stops where the pictures stop: at the
  closest or widest level a pack has there, the button is faded and the header says so. Outside every
  pack the map is white with a dotted frame ("Edge of the map").
- **Long press** a point for its coordinates and **Add bookmark here**.
- **Search** finds towns, districts, streets, stations, parks and places by name (accents and capitals
  do not matter), in every pack.
- **Menu**:
  - **Bookmarks**: tap to go there; long press to rename or delete.
  - **Routes**: GPX files (from a phone app, Komoot, Strava, a hiking site) copied into
    `AppFiles/offlinemap/` are drawn as a bold line: opening one goes to it, all of it on the screen,
    and shows its length (**Fit route** goes back to that view).
  - **Go to coordinates**: `41.0082, 28.9784` or `41°0'29.5"N 28°58'42.2"E`, read off a phone.
  - **Map packs**, **Screen** (portrait or landscape) and **About**.

There is no live position: the reader has no GPS, and the phone apps for other e-ink maps cannot
connect to it.

## Files

`AppFiles/offlinemap/` on the card: the `.map` packs and `.gpx` routes. Private files: `view.txt`
(the last view), `bookmarks.txt`, `settings.txt`.

## Building and tests

`src/` holds the modules (`pack.lua` reads a pack, `view.lua` puts the view together in an offscreen
layer, `choose.lua` picks the pack for each tile, `geo.lua`, `names.lua`, `gpx.lua`, `fold.lua`
generated by `tools/offlinemap_fold.py`) and `main.lua`; `bundle.py` makes `index.lua`.

```
python -I apps/offlinemap/test_offlinemap.py
node site/test/test_offlinemap.mjs
```

The pack format is described in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-offline-map-design.md`.
