# Nonograms — XTOS Licorice mini app (API 2.9)

Picture logic puzzles: fill cells so that every row and column shows its clue, the lengths of its
runs of filled cells in order.

- Sizes **5x5, 8x8, 10x10, 12x12**, 150 puzzles each. Larger boards would make the cells too small
  for a finger on the 480 px screen; clue columns are capped (3 to 5 numbers) for the same reason.
- Every puzzle can be solved line by line, with no guessing, and so has one solution: the bank is
  generated by `make_bank.py` (our own code) from blobby random pictures, keeping only those a line
  solver finishes.
- Tap a cell to fill it, tap again to clear. **Fill / X** switches what a tap does; a long press
  always marks an X. The clues of the row and column last tapped are framed, and a line whose cells
  match its clue has its clue struck through. Every fifth grid line is thicker.
- **Undo**, **Clear** (asks), **Next**, size button; the side buttons go to the previous / next
  puzzle. Half-done puzzles keep their cells (up to 40 per size); solved ones are counted.

Files: `src/main.lua`, `src/bank.lua` (generated), `make_bank.py`, `bundle.py`, `test_nonogram.py`
(`python -I apps/nonogram/test_nonogram.py`, which re-checks all 600 puzzles).
