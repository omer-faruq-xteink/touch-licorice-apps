# Icebound Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** a new XTApp, Icebound 1.0.0: a polar expedition of six named people across a generated, fogged
ice map, one day a turn, with a changing mission, weather, events and four endings.

**Architecture:**
- **`src/main.lua`:** the screens, bundled with gamekit into `index.lua`.
- **Package files under `game/`, loaded with `_G.require`:** the region generator, the travel rules
  (terrain, supplies, weather, sight), the team, Riverstead's event engine (a copy) and this game's
  events, the simulation (start, day, actions, end, score), text, and bots for the balance tests.
- **The map:** the hub's 11 x 6 window and the whole-region Map screen are drawn into offscreen layers
  in chunks after input, never from `on_draw`.

**Tech Stack:** Lua (the device runs 5.5 with 32-bit integers), the gamekit lupa harness, Python tests.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-14-icebound-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- **No commits until the device test passes.** Tasks end on a green test run; Task 10 commits.
- **The package:** `apps/icebound/`. Manifest: `app_id` "icebound", `display_name` "Icebound",
  `version` "1.0.0", `entry` "index.lua", `api` "2.0", `permissions` [] (as Riverstead's).
- **Screen:** 480x800, portrait, 1-bit. One redraw per tap. Under 900 draw commands per frame; at most
  about 1024 per `layer:draw` call; no text drawn into layers; layers drawn only after input, start or
  load, never inside `on_draw` (check `layer:draw`'s second return value, as Riverstead's
  `rebuild_layer` does).
- **Time:** 1000 ms per input callback, 1500 ms for `on_draw`; `pcall(function() ctx.budget:extend() end)`
  before a March, a new expedition and a load.
- **Integers:** every intermediate below 2^31. Copy `apps/riverstead/game/rng.lua`, `save.lua` and
  `events.lua` unchanged.
- **Back:** acts on the up event; inner screens go back a level; the title returns false.
- **Saving:** `expedition.txt`, `chronicle.txt`, `prefs.txt`, written on every tap that changes state.
- **Text:** English; deaths told gently; original names; a fictional region (no real places or people).
- **Lint:** `python -I tools/lualint55.py` passes on every `.lua`.
- **Variation:** everything comes from the seed: the region, the mission (never the last one's), the
  season start, the team, the weather spells, the events and the text variants.
- **Lessons from Riverstead 1.0.0** (carry them in from the start): clear the tap regions before a
  handler runs (no double taps); re-check every action at the moment it is taken; fit every text with
  `kit.width` (Studio's font is wider than the harness's 10 px a character); event buttons name people
  by first name; Back takes an event's `back = true` choice when it has one.

## Shared data model

**Terrain** (`R.TERRAIN`): `'flat'`, `'sastrugi'`, `'ridge'`, `'crevasse'`, `'water'`, `'rock'`
(nunatak), `'slope'`. Impassable: `'water'` (unless frozen: `state.frozen[i]`) and `'rock'`.

**Region** (`game/region.lua`):
```
{seed=, char='coast'|'plateau'|'glacier'|'pack', w=11, h=28, t={[i]=terrain}, up={[i]=true} (slope rises
 northwards), start=i, goal=i, mission='crossing'|'pole'|'lost'|'survey', targets={i...} (survey points,
 or the lost camp), cairns={i...}, caches={[i]={food=,fuel=,dogfood=}} (may be empty)}
```
- `i = (y-1)*11 + x`; y = 1 is the far north (top), the start is in the bottom rows.
- Missions: **crossing**, start bottom, goal (the station) top; **pole**, start (base) bottom, goal (the
  pole) in rows 3-6, then back to the start; **lost**, the camp hidden in rows 4-14, cairns every 3-5
  tiles on a trail from it towards the start, the goal (pick-up) on a coast edge; **survey**, 4 points
  along one side edge, the goal (the ship) on the coast.

**Person:**
```
{id=, name=, role='navigator'|'doctor'|'driver'|'cook'|'mechanic'|'surveyor'|'hunter'|'geologist'|'photographer',
 lv=1..5, trait='steady'|'cheerful'|'sulky'|'brave'|'reckless'|'careful'|'proud'|'nervous', health=0..100,
 cond={frostbite=0..3, snowblind=0..3, exhaustion=0..3, injury=0..3, sickness=0..3}, mood=0..100,
 rel={[id]=-1|0|1}, mem={str...}, alive=true|false, veteran=true|nil, warned=day|nil}
```
- A condition's stage 3 sets `warned` (the day the warning line first showed). A person can die only
  when `day - warned >= 2` and a stage-3 condition still holds.

**State (`expedition.txt`):** (`extras = {samples=, plates=}` and `treat = id|nil` too)
```
{v=1, seed=, diff='easy'|'normal'|'hard', mission=, season='early'|'late', day=1.., days=season length,
 month=day (the event engine's clock, always equal to day), region=, pos=i, route={i...}, seen={[i]=true},
 frozen={[i]=true}, pace='rest'|'steady'|'hard', ration='short'|'normal'|'full', progress=0..(points into the
 next tile), sup={food=,dogfood=,fuel=,dogs=,sledges=}, radio='unused'|'called', rescue_at=day|nil,
 depots={[i]={food=,fuel=,dogfood=}}, phase='out'|'back', found={[i]=true}, team={[id]=person}, next_id=,
 weather={kind='clear'|'overcast'|'wind'|'blizzard'|'whiteout', temp=, wind=, left=days of the spell},
 forecast=kind|nil, flags={}, pending={}, event=nil|{id=,vars=}, last_event=, report=, over=nil|'goal'|'back'|'rescued'|'lost',
 recorded=nil|true}
```

**Prefs (`prefs.txt`):** `{diff=, last_mission=, veteran=nil|person (a survivor of the last expedition)}`.

## Review Focus

1. **The app is closed with the morning event open, or mid-route.** Reopening shows the same event
   with the same names and the same route; nothing is rolled again. Test in Task 8.
2. **A route through tiles that turn out impassable** (open water seen only on arrival, a frozen lead
   thawing). The party stops before the tile, the route is cut there, a line says why, and the party
   never stands on water or rock. Test in Task 3.
3. **The end during a March:** the last person dies, or the season ends, or the rescue arrives, with a
   follow-up event pending. The end comes once, the follow-up is dropped, the score and the chronicle
   entry stay the same over a restart. Test in Task 6.
4. **Supplies at zero:** food, fuel or dog food at 0 never go negative; with no dogs the party
   man-hauls (slow, never stuck); with no sledge it can still walk with what it carries. Test in Task 3.
5. **Layers unavailable** (no `ctx.layers`, or `create` failing): the hub and the Map screen draw a plain
   map with terrain marks under 900 commands, no crash. Test in Task 8 (hub) and Task 9 (Map).

### Task 2: The region

**Files:** create `game/region.lua` and `test_region.py`.

**Interfaces:**
- **Consumes:** the rng.
- **Produces:** `game/region.lua` returns `function(Rng) -> G`, with:
  - `G.make(seed, mission) -> region` (retries child seeds until `G.check` passes);
  - `G.xy(i) -> x, y`, `G.idx(x, y) -> i`, `G.around(i) -> {i...}` (8 neighbours), `G.passable(region, frozen, i) -> bool`;
  - `G.path(region, from, to, cost_fn, known) -> {i...}|nil`: the cheapest path by `cost_fn(i) -> points`
    over passable tiles (Dijkstra, 8 directions), only through `known` tiles when `known` is given;
  - `G.check(region) -> ok, why`;
  - `G.CHARS = {'coast','plateau','glacier','pack'}`, `G.MISSIONS = {'crossing','pole','lost','survey'}`.
- **How a region is made:** the character is picked from the seed; each character weights the terrain:
  coast (water leads and flat), plateau (sastrugi and slope), glacier (crevasse fields and slope), pack
  (ridges and water). Terrain grows from seed points; nunataks in small clusters; 2-4 caches, some empty;
  the mission's targets as in the data model.
- **`G.check`** requires a passable path from the start to the goal and to every target, and back to
  the start for pole and lost; and no target on an impassable tile.

- [ ] **Step 1: Test.** It checks that over seeds 1..1000 for each mission: `G.check` holds; the region
  is deterministic per seed and mission; every character occurs; each character's signature terrain is
  its most common after flat; `G.path` finds the path the check found and returns nil when the goal is
  walled off (a hand-made region); `G.path` with `known` never steps on an unknown tile; making 1000

### Task 3: The rules: travel, supplies, weather, sight

**Files:** create `game/rules.lua` and `test_rules.py`.

**Interfaces:**
- **Consumes:** `G`, the rng.
- **Produces:** `game/rules.lua` returns `function(Rng, G) -> R`, with:
  - `R.TERRAIN`, `R.COST = {flat=4, sastrugi=7, ridge=12, crevasse=5, slope=8}` (points per tile;
    downhill slope 3), `R.PACE = {rest=0, steady=10, hard=14}` (points a day, the starting values);
  - `R.cost(state, i) -> points` (terrain, slope direction);
  - `R.power(state) -> points today`: pace x weather (blizzard 1/3, whiteout 0, wind 3/4) x dogs
    (fewer than 3 per sledge: x2/3; no dogs: man-hauling at x1/2) x the driver's level, never below 1
    unless resting or in a whiteout;
  - `R.sight(state) -> radius` (blizzard/whiteout 1, overcast 2, clear 3; +1 with a navigator of level
    3+; next to a nunatak after a climb 4-5) and `R.reveal(state)`;
  - `R.weather_next(state, r)`: spells of 2-5 days; the season's cold deepens with the day (a late
    season starts colder); with a navigator of level 2+, `state.forecast` names a blizzard or whiteout
    coming tomorrow;
  - **what the roles do in the rules:** the cook saves 1 food in 6; the mechanic mends a broken sledge
    in a camp day (otherwise its load is left); the surveyor halves the crevasse risk; the hunter next
    to open water brings seal meat (food and dog food) on a camp day; the geologist takes a sample on
    each nunatak climbed, the photographer a plate at each target, cairn and climb (`state.extras`,
    counted in the score); the driver's level speeds the dogs;
  - `R.climb(state)`: next to a nunatak, the day is spent climbing (no march); sight 4, or 5 with a
    navigator of level 3+, from the party's tile;
  - `R.step(state, r) -> rep`: move along `state.route` spending power on tile costs (carry `progress`),
    stop before an impassable tile and cut the route with a line, eat (food a person a day by ration,
    dog food a dog a day, fuel by temperature), clamp every stock at 0 recording the real change in
    `rep.delta`, apply the crevasse risk roll (it adds a condition through `rep.falls`, never a death),
    reveal, advance the weather; `rep = {delta={}, moved=tiles, lines={}, falls={}, hunger=bool, cold=bool}`;
  - `R.days_left(state) -> {food=, fuel=, dogfood=}` at the present rations;
  - `R.leave_depot(state, amounts)`, `R.take(state, i)` (a depot or cache on the party's tile).

- [ ] **Step 1: Test.** It checks that:
  - steady on flat moves 2-3 tiles a day with full dogs; hard moves more; rest moves none; a whiteout
    moves none; man-hauling still moves (Review Focus 4);
  - consumption by ration and cold; stocks never go below 0 and `rep.delta` is the real change
    (Review Focus 4);
  - a route into water seen on arrival stops before it, the route is cut and a line is added; the party
    never stands on water or rock over 2000 random routes (Review Focus 2);
  - sight radius by weather and navigator; `R.reveal` marks exactly the tiles within it; a climb next to

  - weather comes in spells (no 1-day spells in 500 days), and the average temperature falls over the
    season;
  - depots: leaving and taking add up; an empty cache gives nothing;
  - integers stay below 2^31 over 100 long runs.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run** `python -I apps/icebound/test_rules.py`. Expected: it fails.
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement `rules.lua`.**
- [ ] **Step 4: Run.** Expected: it passes.

### Task 6: The simulation, missions, actions, the end

**Files:** create `game/sim.lua` and `test_sim.py`.

**Interfaces:**
- **Consumes:** `G`, `R`, `TM`, `E`, `T`, `EL`.
- **Produces:** `game/sim.lua` returns `function(Rng, G, R, TM, E, T, EL) -> S`, with:
  - `S.new(seed, diff, opts) -> state`, `opts = {last_mission=, veteran=}`: the mission is drawn and never

  - `S.set_route(state, path) -> ok, why` (adjacent steps over known passable tiles from `pos`);
  - `S.march(state, r) -> rep`: `R.step`, `TM.day`, mission progress (targets found, the pole reached

    the rescue arriving, then `day` and `month` + 1 and the end checks;
  - actions: `S.leave_depot(state)`, `S.kill_dog(state)`, `S.call_rescue(state)` (rescue in 3-6 days),

  - `S.warnings(state) -> {str...}`: food/fuel/dogfood days left (3 or less, or short of the goal at the

  - `S.ending(state) -> {kind=, level=1..3|nil, title=, lines={}, score=}` and `S.score(state)`;

### Task 7: Bots and balance

**Files:** create `game/bots.lua` and `test_balance.py`; tune the numbers in `rules.lua`, `team.lua`,
`sim.lua` and `event_list.lua`.

**Interfaces:**
- **Consumes:** `S`, `R`, `G`, `TM`.
- **Produces:** `game/bots.lua` returns `function(G, R, TM, S) -> B`, with `B.sensible(state, r)`,
  `B.reckless(state, r)`, `B.fixed(state, r)` and `B.answer(state, r)`; each acts for one day (route,
  pace, rations, treat, actions) before `S.march`.
  - **Sensible:** the cheapest path over known tiles towards the current target (unknown tiles cost
    double when no known path exists); rest in a blizzard or whiteout; short rations when food will not
    reach the goal; treat the worst case; take caches near the path; leave a depot at half-way on pole;
    turn back when supplies cannot reach the goal and can reach the start; call the rescue when neither.
  - **Reckless:** always hard pace, full rations, the straight line, never rests.
  - **Fixed:** steady pace, normal rations, the straight line ignoring terrain costs.
  - **`B.answer`:** the choice that loses no one's health when supplies allow, else the safest.

- [ ] **Step 1: Test** (`test_balance.py`, over 300 regions per difficulty and spread over the four
  missions; time it). It checks that:
  - the sensible bot reaches the goal in 60-85% of Normal runs, more on Easy, fewer on Hard;
  - each mission is won by the sensible bot in at least 40% of its Normal runs;
  - the sensible bot loses nobody in at least 60% of Normal runs;
  - every death in every run (all bots) had a warning line at least 2 days before;
  - the reckless bot wins at least 20 points less often than the sensible one, or loses people at least
    twice as often;
  - the fixed bot reaches the best ending (level 1) in under 50% of runs;
  - every run ends by day `days + 1`; one run takes under 1 s in the harness, a day under 30 ms.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run** `ICEBOUND_RUNS=300 python -I apps/icebound/test_balance.py`.
  Expected: it fails or misses the targets.
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement the bots,** then tune until the targets hold. Don't loosen the targets.
